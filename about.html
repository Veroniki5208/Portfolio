Who is Veronique?

Who am I, really?

Origin story

When I was seven, I found a web game that was nothing more than a big red button labeled "Don't Press It." Of course, I pressed it — again and again. Each response surprised me, and I realized something important: even tiny design decisions can shape how people feel, behave, and explore.

That moment sparked a lifelong curiosity about interaction, systems, and how technology quietly guides our actions.

Who I am as a person

Outside of design, I'm a musician who plays piano, guitar, and violin. I hope to compose my own original soundtracks one day.

I love sci-fi and fantasy worlds — alternate realities where small choices change entire systems. I think that fascination with worldbuilding influences how I approach experience design. I'm deeply curious about how technology blends into everyday life and how interfaces shape the way we move through our world.

Who I am as a professional

As a UX designer, I focus on clarity, confidence, and worldbuilding. I love taking complex systems and shaping them into experiences that feel intuitive and approachable. Whether I'm designing community platforms or tools that guide users through confusing interfaces, my goal is always to reduce friction and help people feel supported.

My work spans user research, interaction design, and emerging AI-driven tools. I enjoy translating abstract needs into concrete, usable interfaces — whether that's simplifying workflows, studying human behavior, or prototyping end-to-end products.

Forward orientation

I'm currently exploring projects that combine UX, technology, and emerging AI tools. I'm excited to grow through full-time roles, collaborative work, and opportunities where I can have real impact.

Ultimately, I hope to help build digital experiences that are accessible, human-centered, and genuinely meaningful.

Let's make something actually useful.

I graduate in June 2027 and am seeking full-time product design, UX, and product management roles. I’m also open to part-time internships during the academic year. If you're working on something that matters to real people, I'd love to talk.
